# LMS legal content review

Prepared 12 November 2026. Public routes: `/terms`, `/privacy`.

These are initial portal policy drafts grounded in the implemented workflows, not a certification of legal compliance. CodeEmy should review the text before treating it as its final contractual/privacy notice. No unsupported refund timetable, jurisdiction clause, response SLA, automatic parental-consent process or immediate storage-erasure guarantee was added.

## Confirm with the business

- Registered operating entity/address and how the CodeEmy Technologies brand relates to it.
- Whether `info.codeemy@gmail.com` is the right privacy/support contact and who handles requests.
- Actual retention periods, backup deletion, provider contracts and configured processing regions.
- Enrollment/refund terms, access duration, eligibility and appeal process.
- Whether under-18 learners are enrolled and how guardian verification is handled before collecting their details.
- Recording notices/permissions and recording retention before real Zoom recording automation is enabled.
- Any additional analytics/marketing tools deployed outside the repository.

The footer and registration notice link to the policies. No clickwrap checkbox, signed acceptance record or parental-consent verification was implemented or claimed. If recorded acceptance is needed, implement versioned server-side acceptance as a separate business-approved workflow.
